# Usability Test Plan: Salesforce Sales Intelligence Assistant

## Header

- **PRD**: `explore/prds/salesforce-sales-intelligence-prd.md`
- **Status**: Draft
- **Last Updated**: 20260806

## Objectives

1. Validate that reps can get useful answers without training. `[OPINION]`
2. Confirm users trust recommendations when source citations are shown. `[OPINION]`
3. Identify friction in accepting or modifying recommended actions. `[OPINION]`

## Participant Profile

- 5 sales representatives from pilot cohort `[OPINION — target number]`
- 2 sales managers `[OPINION — target number]`
- 1 sales operations reviewer `[OPINION]`

## Test Scenarios

| # | Scenario | Success Criteria |
|---|----------|------------------|
| 1 | Ask "What are my top leads this week?" and act on the first recommendation | Completes in < 2 minutes; accepts/edits/rejects recommendation `[OPINION]` |
| 2 | Identify an at-risk high-value deal and explain the reason | Locates deal and cites at least one risk signal `[OPINION]` |
| 3 | Request a quarterly forecast and drill into the highest-risk deal | Forecast displayed; drill-down opens within 3 clicks `[OPINION]` |
| 4 | Verify that pricing guidance does not look like an approved discount | User recognises advisory language `[OPINION]` |

## Metrics

- Task success rate `[OPINION]`
- Time-on-task `[OPINION]`
- Trust rating (1–5) per recommendation `[OPINION]`
- Number of times user manually verifies source records `[OPINION]`
